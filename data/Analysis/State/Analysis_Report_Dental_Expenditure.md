Analysis Report: Dental Service Expenditure CAGR

Summary: Dental services expenditure CAGR was analyzed for 2010-2020 using total expenditure and per capita expenditure. South Dakota ranked first in both measures, while other states appeared among the top five for each measure. These rankings provide supplementary evidence of changes in dental-service spending but do not directly measure dental laboratory revenue or demand.

---

1. Work Completed

Question: How did total and per capita dental services expenditure change across states from 2010 to 2020?

Method:
- Metrics :
    - CAGR of Total Dental Services Expenditure
    - CAGR of Per Capita Dental Services Expenditure
- Data source :
    CMS - Health Expenditures by State of Residence.
- Date Period : 2010-2020
- Formula :
        - CAGR of Total Dental Services Expenditure : (2020 Expenditure / 2010 Expenditure)^(1/10) − 1
        - CAGR of per Capita Dental Services Expenditure :
                (2020 Per Capita Dental Expenditure / 2010 Per Capita Dental Expenditure)^(1/10) − 1
            - Per Capita Dental Service Expenditure:
                Total Dental Services Expenditure / Population
        n : 10 years
- Data Coverage Note : The available state-level dental expenditure data used in this analysis cover 2010-2020. Therefore, this analysis does not capture trends after 2020 and covers a shorter period than the establishment CAGR analysis (2010-2023 and 2018-2023).

---

2. Results

Analysis:
CAGR of Total Dental Services Expenditure

Period:
2010-2020

Result:
1. South Dakota / 5.77 %
2. Florida / 5.44 %
3. Utah / 5.31 %
4. Montana / 5.26 %
5. Arizona / 5.00 %

Analysis:
CAGR of Per Capita Dental Services Expenditure

Period:
2010-2020

Result:
1. South Dakota / 4.88 %
2. Montana / 4.29 %
3. Wyoming / 4.08 %
4. Florida / 4.02 %
5. Mississippi / 3.94 %

---

Interpretation:
- South Dakota ranked first in both total dental services expenditure CAGR and per capita dental services expenditure CAGR for 2010-2020. This indicates that it had the highest growth rates among the states analyzed for both total spending and spending per resident.

- Florida and Montana appeared among the top five states in both measures, suggesting that their relative rankings were strong for both total expenditure growth and per capita expenditure growth. Utah and Arizona ranked among the top five in total dental services expenditure CAGR, while Wyoming and Mississippi ranked among the top five in per capita dental services expenditure CAGR. These differences provide distinct perspectives on state-level spending trends.

- Total dental services expenditure CAGR reflects changes in overall spending, which may be influenced by population growth, changes in service utilization, price changes, and other factors. Per capita expenditure CAGR accounts for population size by expressing expenditure on a per-resident basis, but it does not adjust for inflation.

- Dental services expenditure provides supplementary evidence about the potential dental-service market, but it does not directly measure dental laboratory revenue, the number of dental procedures, or demand for outsourced laboratory cases. The analysis covers 2010-2020 only, a shorter period than the establishment and population trend analyses extending through 2023.
