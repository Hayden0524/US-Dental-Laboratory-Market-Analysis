Analysis Report: Dental Practice Establishment CAGR

Summary: Texas, Idaho, Florida, Nevada, and Montana ranked among the top five states by dental practice establishment CAGR for 2010-2023. For 2018-2023, Delaware, Nevada, Idaho, Montana, and Florida ranked among the top five. These rankings reflect relative changes in establishment counts and do not, by themselves, determine market attractiveness.

---

1. Work Completed

Question: How have the numbers of dental practice establishments changed over time?

Method:
- Metric : Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of dental practice establishment counts.
- Data Source : U.S. Census Bureau, County Business Patterns (CBP)
- Formula : CAGR = (Ending Value / Beginning Value)^(1/n) − 1
- Long-Term (2010-2023) :
        (Dental Practice Establishments in 2023 / Dental Practice Establishments in 2010)^(1/13) − 1
- Recent Five-Year (2018-2023) :
        (Dental Practice Establishments in 2023 / Dental Practice Establishments in 2018)^(1/5) − 1

---

3. Interpretation:

- Idaho, Nevada, and Montana ranked among the top five states by dental practice establishment CAGR in both periods. These states also ranked among the top five in dental laboratory establishment CAGR across both periods, although their laboratory establishment trends differed. By contrast, Utah and Arizona did not rank among the top five in both periods for dental laboratory establishment CAGR. Utah ranked 7th in the long-term period and 35th in the recent five-year period, while Arizona ranked 15th and 7th, respectively.

- Texas ranked first in long-term dental practice establishment CAGR at 2.44%, but its recent five-year CAGR declined to 0.79%, placing it 10th. This indicates that its relative growth position weakened in the recent period.

- Florida ranked among the top five in dental practice establishment CAGR in both periods, with its CAGR increasing from 1.60% to 1.80%. In contrast, its dental laboratory CAGR remained negative and declined from -2.06% to -2.77%. This divergence suggests that dental practice establishments continued to grow while laboratory establishments declined, warranting further examination alongside the Growth Gap results.

- Delaware ranked first in recent dental practice establishment CAGR and also ranked among the top five in dental laboratory establishment CAGR in both periods. Its dental practice establishment growth should therefore be assessed alongside the growth of existing laboratory supply.

- This analysis measures changes in dental practice establishment counts, not patient demand, revenue, or laboratory purchasing behavior.
