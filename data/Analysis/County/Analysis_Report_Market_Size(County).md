Analysis Report: County Market Size

Summary: County market size was assessed across five selected states using 2023 dental practice establishments, dental
         laboratory establishments, and population. Los Angeles County ranked first across all three metrics, while Harris County stood out for its large dental practice base relative to its dental laboratory ranking.

---

1. Work Completed

Question: Which Counties have the largest potential dental service markets based on dental practice establishments, dental laboratory establishments, and population?

Method :
- Metrics :
    - Dental Practice Establishments (2023)
    - Dental Laboratory Establishments (2023)
    - Population (2023)
- Data source :
    - U.S. Census Bureau - County Business Patterns (CBP), 2023
    - U.S. Census Bureau - Population Estimates Program (PEP), Vintage 2023
- Geographic Scope : California, Florida, Idaho, Nevada, and Texas
- Reference Year : 2023
- Ranking Method:
    - Counties were ranked independently for each metric
    - Higher values received higher rankings (Rank 1 = highest value)
    - Unpublished CBP values were excluded from ranking
    - Tied values received the same rank
- Data Handling Note : Blank CBP values were retained as blank cells and were not interpreted as zero establishments.

---

2. Results

Analysis:
Ranking of Dental Practice Establishments

Period:
2023

Result:
1. Los Angeles County / California / 6,420
2. Orange County / California / 2,876
3. Harris County / Texas / 2,302

Analysis:
Ranking of Dental Laboratory Establishments

Period:
2023

Result:
1. Los Angeles County / California / 208
2. Orange County / California / 146
3. Miami-Dade County / Florida / 65

Analysis:
Ranking of Population

Period:
2023

Result:
1. Los Angeles County / California / 9,663,345
2. Harris County / Texas / 4,835,125
3. San Diego County / California / 3,269,973

---

Interpretation:
- Largest Market : Largest Market: LA ranked first in all three metrics, with 6,420 practices, 208 labs, and a population
                   of 9.66m. It represents the largest observed market among the analyzed counties, but also has substantial existing competition.

- Demand and Competition : Harris County, Texas, ranked third in dental practices (2,302) and second in
                           population (4.84 million), while ranking sixth in dental laboratories (44).
                           This difference makes Harris County particulary interesting for further
                           examination of demand relative to existing laboratory supply.

- Data Coverage: Dental practice data were available for 278 of 440 counties, while dental laboratory data were available
                 for only 88. Rankings reflect published observations only; blank values were not treated as zero.
