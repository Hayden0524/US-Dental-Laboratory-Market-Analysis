Analysis Report: Dental Laboratory Trends

Summary : County-level dental laboratory data were insufficient for a reliable longitudinal analysis across the
          five selected states. Due to substantial missing observations, county-level CAGR rankings and Growth gap
          analysis were not conducted

---

1. Work Completed

Question: Is the available County Business Patterns data sufficient to analyze dental laboratory establishments trends
          across the selected counties?

Method :
- Metrics :
    - Published Dental Laboratory Establishments Coverage
    - Number of counties with data available across all reference years
- Data source :
    - U.S. Census Bureau - County Business Patterns (CBP)
- NAICS Code : 339116 (Dental Laboratories)
- Geographic Scope : California, Florida, Idaho, Nevada, and Texas (440 counties)
- Data Period :
  - 2010
  - 2018
  - 2023
- Analytical Decision
  - County-level CAGR, Ranking, and Growth Gap analyses were not conducted due to insufficient data coverage
  - Missing observations were retained as blank and were not interpreted as zero establishments.
- Data Coverage Note:
  - Only 85 counties had published observations across all three reference years.
  - The limited coverage does not support reliable comparisons across the full set of 440 counties.

---

2. Results

Analysis:
Published Dental Laboratory Establishments Data Availability

Period:
2010-2023

Result:
Year | Published Counties | Coverage
|---|---|---|
2010|158|35.9%|
|---|---|---|
2018|95|21.6%|
|---|---|---|
2023|88|20.0%|

- Matched County Coverage:
  - Counties with data in all three years : 85
  - Coverage of selected counties : 19.3 %
  - Counties without complete three-year observations : 355

---

Interpretation:

- Limited Coverage : Published observations declined from 158 counties in 2010 to 88 in 2023, leaving only 20.0 % of
                     the selected counties available for comparison.

- Insufficient Longitudinal Data : Only 85 counties had data across all three reference years, representing 19.3 % of
                                   the target counties. This coverage was considered insufficient for comprehensive
                                   county-level trend rankings.

- Analytical Direction : Dental Laboratory CAGR, Ranking, Growth Gap, and Dental Laboratory based Market Density analysis
                         were excluded. These measures could not reliably represent the overall county-level market
                         due to substantial missing observations.
