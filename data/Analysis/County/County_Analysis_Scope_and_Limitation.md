# County Analysis: Scope and Limitations

## 1. Analysis Purpose

County-level analysis was conducted across 440 counties in Florida, Texas, California, Idaho, and Nevada to examine local market size and potential demand growth.

The analysis aimed to provide a more detailed comparison of potential dental laboratory markets following the initial state-level screening.

## 2. Analysis Completed

- **Market Size:** Compared 2023 population, dental practice establishments, and dental laboratory establishments.
- **Demand Growth:** Evaluated long-term (2010–2023) and recent five-year (2018–2023) population and dental practice growth.
- **Population Trends:** Compared population growth rates and absolute population changes across counties.

## 3. Data Limitations

County-level Dental Laboratory data had substantial missing observations.

- Only 85 of 440 counties (19.3%) had published Dental Laboratory establishment data across all three reference years: 2010, 2018, and 2023.
- Missing observations were retained as blank values and were not interpreted as zero establishments.
- The available coverage was insufficient for reliable county-level Dental Laboratory CAGR, Growth Gap, and laboratory-based market density analysis.
- County-level Dental Services Expenditure data were not available in the datasets reviewed.

## 4. Analytical Decision

Due to insufficient Dental Laboratory data coverage, a comprehensive county-level comparison of market demand and laboratory competition could not be established reliably.

County-level analysis was therefore limited to the metrics supported by available data. The final market screening and location recommendations remained focused on the state level, where the analysis provided a broader basis for comparing market size, growth, and competitive conditions.

## 5. Conclusion

County-level analysis provided additional insight into local market size and demand growth. However, limitations in Dental Laboratory establishment data prevented a reliable assessment of local competition across the selected counties.

These limitations were considered when determining the appropriate geographic scope for the final recommendations.
