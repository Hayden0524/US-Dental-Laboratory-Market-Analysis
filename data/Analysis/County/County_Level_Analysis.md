County-Level Analysis Plan

---

1. Analysis Scope

Analyze counties in Florida, Texas, California, Idaho, and Nevada using County Business Patterns (CBP) data from 2010–2023.

* Reference Year
2023 was selected as the reference year because it is the latest common year available in the CBP analysis period. A single reference year ensures consistent comparison of population, dental practice, and dental laboratories across counties.

---

2. Screening Metrics

- Market Size

Metrics: Population (2023), Dental Practice Establishments (2023), and Dental Laboratory Establishments (2023).

Purpose: Assess the potential customer base and existing laboratory supply. 

- Demand Growth

Metrics: Dental Practice CAGR, Population CAGR, and absolute population change.

Periods: Long-term (2010–2023) and recent five-year (2018–2023).

Purpose: Evaluate growth in the potential customer base.

- Laboratory Trends

Metrics: Dental Laboratory Establishment counts and CAGR, and Growth Gap.

Periods: Long-term (2010–2023) and recent five-year (2018–2023).

Purpose: Assess changes in laboratory supply and the competitive environment.

Analytical Limitation:
    - County-level Dental Laboratory CAGR and Growth Gap analysis were excluded due to insufficient data coverage.
    - Missing observations were retained as blank and were not interpreted as zero establishments.

- Market Density (2023)

Metrics: Population per Dental Practice, Population per Dental Laboratory, and Dental Practices per Dental Laboratory.

Purpose: Compare the relative distribution of customers, practices, and laboratories across counties.

Analytical Limitation: Dental Laboratory-based density metrics were excluded due to insufficient county-level establishment data.

---

3. Data Limitation

- Dental Laboratory Establishments: County-level data coverage was insufficient for reliable longitudinal analysis and laboratory-based market density comparisons.

- Dental Services Expenditure: County-level expenditure data were not available in the datasets reviewed; expenditure metrics were excluded from county-level comparisons.

- Missing Data: Unpublished CBP observations were retained as blank and were not interpreted as zero establishments.
