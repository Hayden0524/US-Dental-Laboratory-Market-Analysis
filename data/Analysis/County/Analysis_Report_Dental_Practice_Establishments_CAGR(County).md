Analysis Report: Dental Practice Establishments CAGR

Summary : Dental practice establishments growth was analyzed across five selected for 2010-2023 and 2018-2023. County rankings identify areas with the strongest long-term and recent growth in potential dental laboratory customer markets.

---

1. Work Completed

Question: How did dental practice establishments change across counties from 2010 to 2023, and which counties experienced the strongest growth?

Method :
- Metrics :
    - CAGR of Dental Practice Establishments (Long-Term)
    - CAGR of Dental Laboratory Establishments (Recent 5 years)
- Data source :
    - U.S. Census Bureau - County Business Patterns (CBP)
- NAICS Code : 621210 (Office of Dentists)
- Geographic Scope : California, Florida, Idaho, Nevada, and Texas
- Data Period :
  - Long-Term (LT) : 2010-2023
  - Recent 5 Years (R5Y) : 2018-2023
- Formula :
  - CAGR_LT: (2023 Establishments / 2010 Establishments)^(1/13) − 1
  - CAGR_R5Y: (2023 Establishments / 2018 Establishments)^(1/5) − 1
- Ranking Method :
  - Counties were ranked independently by CAGR for each period.
  - Higher CAGR values received higher rankings (Rank 1 = highest growth rate)
  - Tied values received the same rank
- Data Coverage Note :
  - CAGR was calculated only when establishment data were available for both the starting and ending years.
  - Blank CBP values were not treated as zero and were excluded from CAGR calculations and rankings.
  - Florida showed a relatively high number of missing observations in the 2023 CBP county-level data. This may
    associated with CBP publication thresholds, but the reason for each missing observation cannot be confirmed
    from the public dataset alone.
  - Consequently, the number of Florida counties available for comparison may be limited.

2. Results

Analysis:
CAGR of Dental Practice Establishments (Long-Term)

Period:
2010-2023

Result:
1. Waller County / Texas / 10.51 %
2. Chambers County / Texas / 8.82 %
2. Karnes County / Texas / 8.82 %
2. Pecos County / Texas / 8.82 %

* Note : Counties with the same CAGR when rounded to two decimal places were assigned the same rank.

Analysis:
CAGR of Dental Practice Establishments (Recent 5 Years)

Period:
2018-2023

Result:
1. Waller County / Texas / 17.08 %
2. Lemhi County / Idaho / 10.76 %
3. San Patricio County / Texas / 8.92 %

---

Interpretation:

- Growth Leaders : Waller County, Texas, was the only county appearing in the top 3 for both periods, with 10.51% LT CAGR
                   and 17.08% R5Y CAGR. Other leading counties differed between periods, suggesting that the strongest
                   growth locations depend on the timeframe examined.
                
- Growth Slowdown : Among 273 counties with data for both periods, 165 recorded lower R5Y CAGR than LT CAGR, while
                    93 recorded higher rates. The median CAGR declined from 0.97% to 0.00%, indicating that dental
                    practice growth generally slowed in recent years.

- Growth Rate vs Market Scale : Waller County's Practice increased from 3 to 11 (+8), despite its highest LT CAGR.
                                In contrast, Los Angeles County increased by 845 practices (5,575 to 6,420) and
                                Harris County by 653 (1,649 to 2,302), showing that high growth rates do not necessarily represent the largest expansion in potential customers.
