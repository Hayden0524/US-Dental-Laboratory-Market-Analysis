Analysis Report: County Population Growth

Summary : Population growth was analyzed across 440 counties in five selected states for 2010-2023 and 2018-2023
          using CAGR and absolute population change. Texas counties led the growth-rate rankings, while the largest
          population gains were in major counties such as Harris and Collin.

---

1. Work Completed

Question: How has population changed across counties from 2010 to 2023, and which counties experienced the strongest
          growth?

Method :
- Metrics :
    - Population CAGR (Long-Term)
    - Population CAGR (Recent 5 Years)
    - Absolute Population Change (Long-Term)
    - Absolute Population Change (Recent 5 Years)
- Data source :
    - U.S. Census Bureau - County Intercensal Population Estimates, 2010-2020 (CO-EST2020INT-POP)
    - U.S. Census Bureau - Population Estimates Program, Vintage 2023 (CO-EST2023-ALLDATA)
- Geographic Scope : California, Florida, Idaho, Nevada, and Texas (440 counties)
- Data Period :
  - Long-Term (LT) : 2010-2023
  - Recent 5 Years (R5Y) : 2018-2023
- Formula :
  - CAGR_LT : (2023 Establishments / 2010 Establishments)^(1/13) − 1
  - CAGR_R5Y : (2023 Establishments / 2018 Establishments)^(1/5) − 1
  - Population Change : Ending Population - Starting Population
- Ranking Method :
  - Counties were ranked independently by CAGR for each period
  - Higher CAGR values received higher rankings (Rank 1 = highest growth rate)
  - Tied Values received the same rank based on CAGR values rounded to two decimal places.
  - Absolute population change was compared using actual population increases without a seperate ranking column.
- Data Coverage Note :
  - Population data were available for all 440 counties across the three reference years.
  - 2010 and 2018 data were obtained from 2010-2020 Intercensal Estimates, while 2023 data came from Vintage 2023.
  - Different reference data and estimation vintages were used due to limitations in publicly available county-level
    data. Therefore, the results should be interpreted as indicative population growth trends rather than perfectly
    aligned estimates.

---

Interpretation:

- Growth Concentration : All three highest-ranked counties in both CAGR periods were located in Texas. Kaufman County
                         led both (4.56 % LT and 7.13 % R5Y), while Comal County ranked second in both, indicating
                         sustained populatino growth in these areas.
                
- Growth Rate vs Population Gains : Kaufman County recorded the highest growth rate but added 81,717 residents over
                                    the long term. In contrast, Harris County gained 727,664 residents, while
                                    Collin County led recent population gains with 188,571, demonstrating the
                                    difference between proportional growth and actual market expansion.

- Regional Differences : Recent population trends varied considerably across states. California experienced population
                         declines in 36 of 58 counties, while 43 of 44 Idaho counties and 55 of 67 Florida counties
                         recorded population increases.
