Methodology

This document describes the analytical methods used to evaluate the U.S. dental laboratory market and identify potential locations for a new dental laboratory.

1. Analysis Framework

The analysis was conducted at two geographic levels: State and County.

State-Level Analysis: Screen U.S. states based on market size, industry growth, population trends, market density, and dental healthcare expenditure.

County-Level Analysis: Examine market size and demand growth in selected states to identify potential local markets.

The analysis focused on two industries:

NAICS 339116: Dental Laboratories

NAICS 621210: Offices of Dentists

2. State-Level Analysis

State-level screening evaluated market conditions using the following indicators.

2.1 Market Size

Dental Laboratory Establishments

Dental Practice Establishments

Population

Purpose: Assess the overall market scale and the potential customer base in each state.

2.2 Industry Growth

Growth trends were measured using the Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR).

Long-Term CAGR (LT): 2010–2023

Recent 5-Year CAGR (R5Y): 2018–2023

Calculation:

CAGR = (Ending Value / Beginning Value) ^ (1 / Number of Years) - 1

The analysis compared the growth of dental practices and dental laboratories to evaluate changes in market demand and industry supply.

2.3 Growth Gap

Growth Gap measures the difference between dental practice growth and dental laboratory growth.

Calculation:

Growth Gap = Dental Practice CAGR - Dental Laboratory CAGR

A positive Growth Gap indicates that dental practice establishments grew faster than dental laboratory establishments over the same period.

Both long-term and recent growth patterns were considered.

2.4 Population Trends

Population trends were evaluated using:

Population CAGR (2010–2023)

Recent Population CAGR (2018–2023)

Absolute Population Change

Purpose: Assess long-term and recent population growth as an indicator of potential market demand.

2.5 Market Density

Market density indicators were used to evaluate the relationship between population, dental practices, and dental laboratories.

Population per Dental Practice

Population per Dental Laboratory

Dental Practices per Dental Laboratory

Purpose: Compare the relative concentration of potential customers and existing laboratory supply across states.

2.6 Dental Healthcare Expenditure

State-level dental services expenditure data from CMS (2010–2020) was used as a supplementary market indicator.

Per Capita Dental Services Expenditure was calculated by dividing total dental services expenditure by state population.

Purpose: Assess broader dental healthcare spending patterns across states.

This indicator does not directly measure dental laboratory revenue or demand.

2.7 State Screening and Candidate Selection

States were evaluated using a combination of market size, industry growth, demand trends, market density, and supplementary expenditure indicators.

The screening process considered both long-term and recent trends rather than relying on a single metric or population size alone.

The results were used to identify primary candidate states and reserve candidates for further investigation.

3. County-Level Analysis

County-level analysis was conducted in five selected states:

Florida

Texas

California

Idaho

Nevada

A total of 440 counties were included in the county-level screening.

3.1 Market Size

Market size was assessed using 2023 data to maintain consistency with the county-level CBP reference year.

Indicators included:

Population (2023)

Dental Practice Establishments (2023)

Dental Laboratory Establishments (2023)

Purpose: Evaluate the potential customer base and existing laboratory supply at the county level.

3.2 Demand Growth

Demand growth was evaluated using:

Dental Practice Establishment CAGR

Population CAGR

Absolute Population Change

Both long-term (2010–2023) and recent (2018–2023) trends were considered where data was available.

3.3 Dental Laboratory Trends

County-level dental laboratory establishment data was reviewed to examine industry presence and historical trends.

However, data availability was insufficient to support reliable longitudinal comparisons across all counties.

Therefore, county-level Dental Laboratory CAGR and Growth Gap were not used for ranking or candidate selection.

3.4 County Screening Considerations

Counties with fewer than three establishments or missing data were treated cautiously in market-size comparisons.

Missing or suppressed CBP values were retained as missing values rather than interpreted as zero.

County-level findings were used as supplementary evidence to assess local market conditions within the selected states.

4. Data Handling and Limitations

The analysis was subject to the following data limitations:

CBP establishment data may be suppressed or unavailable for certain geographic areas and industries.

County-level dental laboratory data coverage was insufficient for reliable longitudinal growth analysis.

County-level dental services expenditure data was not available for this analysis.

Population datasets differ by geographic level and estimation period.

CMS dental services expenditure represents broader dental healthcare spending, not dental laboratory-specific revenue.

Due to these limitations, county-level conclusions were restricted to the indicators supported by available data.

5. Final Assessment

State-level analysis served as the primary basis for identifying potential market locations.

County-level analysis provided additional insight into market size and demand growth within the selected states.

The final assessment considered the combined evidence from both geographic levels while acknowledging data coverage limitations and the distinction between broader dental healthcare spending and dental laboratory market demand.
